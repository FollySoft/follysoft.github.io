December 24, 2024

I'm starting this post 3 months early, because I don't know what else to do with myself. Not long ago, I was contacted by Sebastián Gioseffi, one of the organizers of the Experimental Games Workshop at GDC. He had strongly encouraged me to submit The Factory Reset Jam for cosnideration to be presented at the workshop. The fact he contacted me AT ALL was huge for me - I never felt any of my work was really suited for events or presentations like EGW. But I was stoked beyond belief that out of everything, a litte 2-week jam I hosted, that brought out such incredible creativity from it's participants, had a shot at being shared with the industry at large.

Fast forward about a month, and the congratulatory email comes in. Then I'm joining a discord server, with so many other members whose work had inspired my own. Then I'm writing a bio and submitting a headshot. And now, I'm looking at my own face on the GDC website, my own name next to the names of creators whose work I've spent so many hours consuming, in absolute awe of their artistry.

How in the hell did I get here?
